## 🏛️ Executive Summary

National AI Safety Institutes (such as the UK AISI, US AISI, and Singapore’s Project Moonshot) face an existential **enforcement gap**: they rely on voluntary pre-deployment testing agreements. Frontier labs can refuse access or bypass red lines during competitive races.

**Project SELAT** solves this problem by proving that **middle powers do not need to build frontier models from scratch to govern them**. By gatekeeping physical infrastructure choke points—specifically **3.24 GW of hyperscale AI data centers in Johor** and **13% of global semiconductor packaging (OSAT) in Penang**—Malaysia can unilaterally enforce mandatory zero-knowledge compute verification and pre-deployment safety red lines across both US and Chinese frontier labs.

## ⚡ The Asymmetric Physical Choke Points

1. **Johor 3.24 GW Safeguarded Compute Zone:**
   When Singapore capped domestic data center power (<1.4 GW), global hyperscalers invested $18B+ into Johor, Malaysia (2 km across the causeway). Johor uniquely co-hosts both US frontier clouds (Microsoft, AWS, Oracle, Nvidia/YTL) and Chinese hyperscalers (ByteDance/TikTok anchor at Bridge DC, GDS) on the **same national power grid**.
2. **Penang & Kulim Advanced Packaging Hub:**
   Malaysia commands **13% of global back-end chip assembly, test, and packaging (ATP/OSAT)** and >20% of U.S. semiconductor imports. Every frontier accelerator (Nvidia B200, AMD MI325) requires advanced packaging before deployment.

## 🛡️ Core Verification Architecture (IAEA Parallels)

Borrowing from **IAEA INFCIRC/66** nuclear safeguards, Malaysia does not inspect proprietary model weights during training: **300 MW of synchronized GPU gradient steps cannot hide from a state power utility**.

* **Layer 1 (TNB Grid Harmonics):** Out-of-band substation sensors measure high-frequency power fluctuations outside transformer perimeters (0% customer payload inspection). Sustained flatline power draws and periodic 45-second notches detect undeclared $>10^{26}$ FLOP training runs.
* **Layer 2 (Fabric Attestation):** Read-only switch graph hashes verify that multi-tenant cloud halls remain isolated ($\le 512$ GPUs/pod).
* **Layer 3 (Confidential Escrow):** Completed checkpoints remain cryptographically locked in local on-premise confidential enclaves until passing joint NAIO + Singapore/UK AISI evaluations (Project Moonshot + Equistamp).
* **Physical Circuit Breaker:** Contractual remedy under Clause 14.2 of Tenaga Nasional Berhad's (TNB) Green Lane Power Supply Agreement steps the substation down to a 20% maintenance cap immediately upon verified breach, without court injunction delays.
